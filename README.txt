Dastarkhan – Family Food Planner (free, standalone)

Host it free (Netlify): sign up at https://app.netlify.com/signup with your PERSONAL email,
then drag this "dastarkhan" folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop (or onto your site's Deploys page to update).
GitHub Pages: upload the files inside this folder to a public repo, then Settings > Pages > main branch.

Install on phones: Android Chrome menu > Install app; iPhone Safari Share > Add to Home Screen.
Own food items: Family > Food items & prices.  Own dishes: Recipes > New dish (choose a season).
Hide a built-in dish: open it > Hide from my list.  Use only your dishes: Recipes > Plan with my dishes only.
Season & winter energy: Family > Season.
Data stays on each phone. Use Family > "Save / send backup" to share or move it.
